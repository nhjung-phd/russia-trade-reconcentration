# Key results

This file is a navigation aid, not a substitute for the manuscript.

1. **Import re-concentration:** China’s share of Russian imports rises strongly after 2022. The direction survives balanced-partner and BoP-denominator sensitivity checks, although the magnitude is denominator-sensitive.
2. **Product-level breadth:** The HS6 distribution shifts broadly toward China on the import side; the HS4-cluster bootstrap interval remains well above zero.
3. **Export asymmetry:** Aggregate exports reorient toward China and India, but HS6 breadth is much weaker than on the import side; India’s change is strongly energy-intensive.
4. **Absolute exposure gap:** The absolute Russia–China import-side exposure gap (percentage-point difference) widens under both BACI and BoP-denominator specifications. Under the BoP denominator, however, ratio and log-ratio change only slightly, so the robust RQ3 claim is limited to the widening of the absolute gap.
