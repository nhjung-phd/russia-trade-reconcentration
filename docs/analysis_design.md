# Analysis Design

## Research design

This is a **non-causal structural empirical analysis** of Russia's trade reorientation after 2022.

The study does not estimate the causal effect of sanctions. The empirical objective is to characterize whether post-2022 trade reorientation was accompanied by partner re-concentration, product-level dependence, and widening bilateral trade exposure asymmetry.

## Time windows

- Full annual sample: 2015–2024
- Pre-period: 2019–2021
- Adjustment year: 2022
- Post-period: 2023–2024

## Main analyses

1. Partner-share trajectories
2. Trade concentration: HHI, CR1, CR3, Shannon entropy, effective number of partners
3. China specificity relative to India and Türkiye
4. HS6-level dependence thresholds (China share ≥50%, ≥70%, ≥90%)
5. Non-energy robustness (excluding HS27)
6. Value–quantity robustness for HS270900
7. Russia–China bilateral trade exposure gap
8. Exploratory structural-break screening
9. HS6-level statistical inference:
   - Wilcoxon signed-rank test
   - Bootstrap 95% confidence intervals
   - Sign-flip permutation test
   - Rank-biserial effect size
   - China–EU share-change correlation

## Interpretation rule

Use structural language such as:

- `after 2022`
- `post-2022`
- `was associated with`
- `coincided with`
- `re-concentration`

Do **not** interpret the estimates as the causal effect of sanctions.
