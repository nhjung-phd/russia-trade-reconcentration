# Results guide

## Primary evidence

The principal empirical evidence is provided by:

- partner-share changes;
- country-level concentration;
- EU27-bloc concentration;
- balanced-partner sensitivity;
- HS6 distributional changes;
- EU27-China product-level co-movement;
- Bank of Russia denominator sensitivity.

## Auxiliary evidence

Longitudinal HS6 profiles and revised PPML-HDFE are auxiliary. The corrected import PPML model did not converge and should not be represented as a valid estimated result.

## Interpretation

The corrected results support a distinction between robust import-side re-concentration and export destination reorientation whose concentration effect depends on whether EU27 members are treated separately or as one geopolitical bloc.
