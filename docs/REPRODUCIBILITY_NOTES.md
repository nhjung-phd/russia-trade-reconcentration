# Reproducibility notes

## Fixed period definition

- Full period: 2015-2024
- Pre-period: 2019-2021
- Adjustment year: 2022
- Post-period: 2023-2024

## EU27 definition

The current EU27 composition is applied consistently through the study period. France is represented by code 251 in this analytical coding. Italy is resolved dynamically from the input dataset as 380 or 381. The United Kingdom is excluded from the current EU27 definition.

## Two concentration definitions

1. Country-level: every EU27 member remains an individual trading partner.
2. EU27-bloc: EU27 members are combined into one geopolitical bloc before concentration is calculated.

Both are reported because they answer different substantive questions.

## Inference limits

The study is structural and longitudinal rather than a causal identification design. War, sanctions, corporate exits, commodity-price shocks, payment changes, logistics changes, and Russian countermeasures overlap after 2022.

## PPML

The revised PPML is auxiliary. The corrected import model did not converge under the revised high-dimensional fixed-effect specification. This failure is preserved for transparency.
